Musical journey

Maty Poch is an Argentine guitarist, songwriter and independent music producer whose career has developed primarily within the blues and rock scene.

Early years

From listener to guitarist

Growing up with 1980s rock and pop, Poch found a defining influence in AC/DC's Let There Be Rock. He worked outside school hours to buy his first electric guitar, a Faim, and initially taught himself through chord books, riffs and songs learned by ear.

Music education

José Hernández Music School

He later studied electric guitar and music theory at the José Hernández Municipal Music School in Vicente López, Buenos Aires. Its focus on popular music, jazz and swing shaped his approach to performance and arrangement.

His technical background in electronics and telecommunications also became part of his musical work: he built some of his own equipment and explored digital recording at a time when home recording was still uncommon.

1994 – mid-2000s

Expreso Rock

In 1994 he co-founded Expreso Rock, a band rooted in energetic guitar-driven rock. For more than a decade the group played motorcycle gatherings, festivals, benefit concerts and venues in Buenos Aires and elsewhere in Argentina.

Poch played guitar and sang backing vocals, sharing lead and rhythm guitar duties with Maximiliano Talarico. The band recorded an independent five-song album and contributed two more recordings to a compilation. Surviving recordings and photographs are preserved in this website's archive.

Later work

Session work and arrangements

After Expreso Rock, Poch played in other live projects and worked on session recordings and arrangements.

2009 – 2024

E.A.H. — Estamos al Horno

What began around 2009 as informal rehearsals among colleagues grew into E.A.H. (Estamos al Horno), a hard rock and blues-rock band active until December 2024. Its repertoire was distinguished by reinterpretations and arrangements of rock and blues songs.

The core lineup featured Gustavo Moffa on vocals and harmonica, Diego Moffa on bass and Maty Poch on guitar and backing vocals, alongside several drummers over the years. Recordings include Corazón de motor and Revancha.

Today

Independent songwriting and production

Alongside the final years of E.A.H., Poch and Moffa began developing a project focused on blues. Songs that did not fit that project became part of Poch's independent catalog.

En Mi Casa was his first release under his own name. His current work blends blues, blues-rock and rock, with guitar, composition, arrangements and production at its center. Most of his music is produced in his own studio, alongside audiovisual projects created for his original songs.
